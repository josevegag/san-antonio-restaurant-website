# Official content review

Checked 2026-09-27. The client's official website is the authority for business information.
The legacy Wix pages below were consulted before the production migration on 2026-09-27. Their old URLs now redirect to equivalent pages on this site; the former Wix content is no longer available at those addresses. These links record where the published facts were originally checked.

## Verified sources

- [Official website](https://www.sanantoniomr.com/): restaurant name, three location claim and online delivery announcement.
- [Official contact page](https://www.sanantoniomr.com/contact-5): addresses, phones and opening hours for Monroe, Broadway and Clifton. It labels Broadway “Restaurant 3” even though Clifton is also “Restaurant 3”; this project uses neighborhood names from the ordering page. Monroe's weekend time is printed `9:3 0 am`, interpreted as 9:30 AM in line with Monday to Friday.
- [Official ordering page](https://www.sanantoniomr.com/online-ordering): individual Monroe, Broadway and Clifton order links.
- [Monroe online menu](https://order.epipay.com/m4200000556620/en/WebOrder?STORE_CODE=MTAwMA%3D%3D), [Broadway online menu](https://order.epipay.com/m4200000556620/en/WebOrder?STORE_CODE=MTAwMQ%3D%3D), [Clifton online menu](https://order.epipay.com/m4200000556620/en/WebOrder?STORE_CODE=MTAwMg%3D%3D): selected product names, categories and branch-specific prices. Full live catalogs remain linked from the website. Duplicate items with differing prices were omitted from the local price display.
- [Official story](https://www.sanantoniomr.com/ourstory): family ownership, made-to-order Mexican food, recipes passed through generations and more than ten years serving customers.
- [Official party promotions](https://www.sanantoniomr.com/promociones-para-fiestas): five party packages, listed prices and five-day advance notice.

## Items to confirm with the client

1. **Opening hours:** The official contact page and the linked ordering provider differ in places. The website displays the contact page hours. Confirm the final hours for all three restaurants, especially Broadway and Monroe.
2. **Social media:** The visible Facebook, Twitter and Instagram icons on the official website link to Wix accounts, not the restaurant. Request actual profile URLs before publishing social links.
3. **Reservations:** The official website has no confirmed reservation platform, policy or booking URL. This website asks guests to call their chosen restaurant. Confirm whether reservations are accepted, party size rules and any desired booking provider.
4. **Catering orders:** Party packages and prices are published, but no reliable direct ordering destination or catering contact is shown. Confirm which branch or number handles catering and whether packages are valid at all three locations.
5. **Photo attribution:** The supplied 81 photographs are sorted by dish, not by restaurant. Branch pages show these as general restaurant photography and state that branch attribution is unconfirmed. Request location-specific interior/exterior photos to create true branch galleries.
6. **Videos:** No original video files were in the supplied folder. Hero and promotional video slots are ready for the client's files.
7. **Menu copy:** The online ordering menus rarely give product descriptions. Brief descriptions in both languages are editorial labels based on the product name and image; confirm detailed ingredient, allergen and dietary copy before adding it.
8. **Brand assets:** The official logo is available only as a small transparent PNG on the current website. Request a high-resolution transparent PNG or vector master for sharper large-scale social graphics and print use.

The official `/menu` page currently contains an unrelated template “Dinner Menu” with ravioli, tuna sashimi and other items. This project does not use it as restaurant menu data. The official ordering links provide the relevant Mexican menu.
